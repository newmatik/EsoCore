# BOM: Air Quality Sensor

The air quality sensor module bill of materials covers the metal-oxide gas sensor, particulate
matter detector, temperature and humidity compensation circuitry, RS-485 transceiver, and
supporting passives. Components are selected for continuous ambient air monitoring in factory
environments. For specifications, see the
[Air Quality Sensor Module](/docs/sensors/air-quality-sensor) documentation.
