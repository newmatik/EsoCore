# BOM: Acoustic Sensor

The acoustic sensor module bill of materials covers the MEMS microphone, low-noise audio
amplifier, anti-aliasing filter network, RS-485 transceiver, and supporting passives. Components
are selected for reliable operation in industrial noise environments. For specifications and
