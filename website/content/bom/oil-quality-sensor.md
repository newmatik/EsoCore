# BOM: Oil Quality Sensor

The oil quality sensor module bill of materials includes the dielectric constant sensing element,
temperature compensation circuitry, precision ADC, RS-485 transceiver, and supporting passives.
Components are chosen for long-term immersion in lubricant and hydraulic fluid environments. For
specifications, see the
[Oil Quality Sensor Module](/docs/sensors/oil-quality-sensor) documentation.
